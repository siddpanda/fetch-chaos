Fetch Chaos — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Fetch Chaos ("the Game") is a casual mobile game developed by Siddhartha Panda. This policy explains what data the Game does — and does not — collect.

Data we collect

None. Version 0.1 of Fetch Chaos collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no device identifiers. The Game does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to play.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves only your best score, stored locally on your device (browser/app local storage). This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Advertising and in-app purchases

Version 0.1 contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds either, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Third parties

The Game does not send data to any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks. If you use the optional "Share Score" feature, sharing happens through your device's built-in share sheet and is governed by the privacy policy of the app you choose to share with.

Children

Because the Game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details listed on the Fetch Chaos Google Play store listing.
